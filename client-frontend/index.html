Welcome to PrivInsure

Privacy-preserving insurance eligibility matching using Multi-Party Computation

How it works:

  1. Open this page in two separate browser windows or tabs
  2. In one window: Click "I'm an Insurance Provider" and set your policy criteria
  3. In the other window: Click "I'm a User" and enter your health information
  4. Both parties submit their data
  5. The system securely computes eligibility without revealing private data
  6. Both parties see the result: eligible or not eligible

🔒 Privacy Guaranteed: Neither party learns the other's private information, only the final eligibility result.
